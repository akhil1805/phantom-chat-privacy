Privacy Policy — Phantom Chat

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data we collect

  • Email address — used for account creation and login
  • Username — used to identify users within the app
  • Device identifier — a randomly generated ID used for account security and session management
  • Push notification token — used to deliver message notifications

2. Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect your real name, phone number, location, contacts, or browsing history.
We do not use analytics or advertising tracking tools.

3. How your messages are protected

All messages are end-to-end encrypted on your device before being sent.
We store only encrypted data and cannot read message content.

4. Data storage and services

  • Supabase — database services
  • Cloudflare R2 — media and file storage
  • Expo — push notification delivery

Only encrypted or necessary operational data is stored with these services.

5. Data retention

Messages and conversations are automatically deleted from our servers after 24 hours.
If a chat is ended early, all related messages, media, and encryption keys are deleted immediately.

6. Your rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.
